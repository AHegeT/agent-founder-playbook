# Toku M.

**Role:** Executive Coach
**Background:** Former Zen Monk

## Story

Toku is a coach for executives with a background as a Zen monk. He is reinventing his coaching approach by extracting insights from the sessions with his clients, creating custom dashboards for them to track their progress and to give them access to the frameworks he uses.

Instead of replacing himself with AI, he is finding creative ways to help his clients further.

## Current Challenge

His current challenge is making what he builds for one client accessible to all, in a secure and impactful manner.
